Content List is a personal website for me to organise and keep track of my anime, games, and movies, all in one place.
I was facing difficulties in keeping track of what anime I had watched, or was watching, or which of them are in continuation. So I thought why not make a little website to handle this stuff easily?
I used hardcoded data instead of any database etc because it was a personal tool. So I thought why do authorization and all that headache.
It's built around my collection, but anyone can customise it by replacing the hardcoded data with their own.

Ratings on each data is not my own
For movies and anime, I used IMDb Ratings
For books, I used goodreads Ratings
And for games, I used an average of IGN, Steam, Xbox and IMDb Ratings (only used IMDb ratings where considerable amount of people had rated)
